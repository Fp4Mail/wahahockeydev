WAHA is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit powered by local families, volunteers, and community support. Donations help us provide programs, equipment, ice time, and scholarships for Westborough players.

## Where support can make a difference

- Player equipment and safety gear
- Skills clinics and development opportunities
- Practice and program ice time
- Need-based support for local families
- High-school hockey initiatives

Every contribution helps. We don’t publish fixed dollar-to-impact claims because costs and program needs change from season to season.

## Other ways to help

Local businesses can ask about sponsorships, families can donate usable equipment, and volunteers can give their time at events or behind the scenes.
