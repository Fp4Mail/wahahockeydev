## Hockey has a home in Westborough

The Westborough Amateur Hockey Association supports local youth and high-school hockey through programs, equipment, ice time, and scholarships. Our goal is simple: create more opportunities for Westborough players to learn the game, develop as teammates, and stay connected to their community.

## Powered by volunteers

WAHA depends on people who care about local hockey. Volunteers support events, communications, fundraising, player programs, and the many small jobs that keep a season moving.
