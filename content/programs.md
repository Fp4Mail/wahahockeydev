WAHA supports players at different stages of their hockey journey. Program availability and details may change by season, so contact us if you need help finding the right fit.

<div class="program-grid">
  <div class="program-card"><h3>Learn to skate & play</h3><p>A welcoming starting point for new players learning balance, movement, and the basics of hockey.</p></div>
  <div class="program-card"><h3>Youth skills clinics</h3><p>Focused instruction that helps developing players improve skating, puck skills, and game awareness.</p></div>
  <div class="program-card"><h3>High-school support</h3><p>Support for local high-school hockey, including equipment stipends and off-ice development.</p></div>
  <div class="program-card"><h3>Scholarships</h3><p>Need-based assistance designed to help make hockey more accessible to Westborough families.</p></div>
</div>
